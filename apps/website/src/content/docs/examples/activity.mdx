The tenant's events as people read them. `memento simulate` plays a small team working a ticket
desk, memento renders every event with its Handlebars template, and the page lists the
sentences, newest first, live.

```txt
memento simulate ──POST /events──▶ memento ──POST /udc/list {collection: events}──▶ feed
  (a ticket desk, 1 event/3 s)      renders  ◀── X-Memento-Locale: en | sr ────────  (EN / SR)
                                             ──ws /memento/ws─────────────────────▶ refetch
```

## What it reads

The `events` UDC collection, every event of the tenant except the CPU monitor's samples:

- filter `{field: "event_type", op: "neq", value: "CPU_LOAD"}`;
- sort `occurred_at desc`;
- infinite paging by offset, 30 rows a page: the next page loads when the end of the list
  scrolls into view, or on **Older events**.

The rows come through `useUdcCollection` of `@jsudc/react`. Each line has the time, the actor's
initials avatar (`initials` and `hue` of `@jsmemento/react`) and id, the **rendered** text, the
instance as a chip (`ticket/T-17`) that opens its [timeline](/examples/entity/), and the event
type as a muted tag.

## The language is the header's

The app's `UdcClient` sends `X-Memento-Locale: en` or `sr` with every request, and memento
renders `rendered` in that locale; stored rows are never changed, the response is. Switching
EN / SR invalidates every UDC list, so the feed comes back in the other language without a
reload. The choice is kept in `localStorage` (`udc-locale`). Which template a sentence comes
from: [Rendering and translations](/concepts/rendering/).

## Live

`useLive` (from `@jsmemento/ui-shell`) follows the whole stream; every event that is not a
`CPU_LOAD` refetches the feed, at most once per 250 ms. New lines fade in.

With no simulator running the feed is empty: the page says what pushes these events and gives
the compose command, `docker compose -f docker-compose.yaml --profile demo up -d simulator`.

The page: [`apps/examples/src/examples/activity/`](https://github.com/digital-cube/jsmemento/tree/main/apps/examples/src/examples/activity).
